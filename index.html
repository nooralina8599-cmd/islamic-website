Daily Islamic Duas 🤲

1. Khana Khane Se Pehle

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

Bismillah

Meaning: Allah ke naam se shuru karta hoon.

2. Khana Khane Ke Baad

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

Alhamdulillah

Meaning: Sab tareef Allah ke liye hai.

3. Ilm Mein Izafe Ki Dua

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

Rabbi zidni ilma

Meaning: Aye mere Rab! Mere ilm mein izafa farma.

4. Duniya Aur Aakhirat Ki Bhalai Ki Dua

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina azaban-nar.

Meaning: Aye hamare Rab! Hamein duniya aur aakhirat mein bhalai ata farma aur aag ke azaab se bacha.

جزاك الله خيرا
